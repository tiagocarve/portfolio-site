musica mundana — Autumn on Brook Street, Secretcape, Mayfair

9 – 13 September 2019 | Júlia Rodrigues + Tiago Esteves

3-channel sound installation: soundscape 1 (room 1), 1h3m loop / soundscape 2 (room 2), 1h5m loop / sound sculpture (hall). These compositions include sounds recorded by Tiago Esteves and sounds uploaded to the Aporee soundmaps, licensed under creative commons. Sound sculpture materials: graphite, charcoal, water and Manhattan 005 by SAHCO fibres on copper plates.

Window Gallery, Canada Walk, Canary Wharf

29 May – 24 June 2011 | Elisabeth Bond

Elisabeth’s woodcuts derive from her observation of the world around her. Pieces of wood are found all over – left by workmen, thrown onto the street. CUTTING LONDON is a response to the wood itself and to the city. Crows is a sound collage of a poem written by Elisabeth Bond for her window installation of prints at Canary Wharf shopping mall. This sound piece follows the idea of intense repetition of sounds and words and played as a 24-hour non-stop loop during an entire month.

Arts Festival

18 June – 23 June 2009 | Soundscapes for an installation by Muna Abu-Qaoud

Muna’s initial idea was to have two different sound pieces: one with speech and another with noises. Her art installation consisted of wood doors that were arranged as a wall separating two different abstract realities. The visitor would listen to different sounds from each side of the installation. Following Muna’s request, Tiago C recorded selected poems read by different people and added a call to prayer recording to the background. For the second part, Tiago C was given total freedom to experiment with electric guitar noises, feedback, and reversed speech.

RadioIN RadioOUT

16 June 2009 (UEL) | with Júlia Rodrigues

RadioIN RadioOUT is a collage of speech and noises from an old radio. The piece was playing in a loop from two small speakers installed inside the open back of an old radio which was itself the living scenario for a clay character sculptured by Julia R. The piece also features a song played on an old piano. The radio speech, including Portuguese spoken parts, is the result of pure chance. (radio box - 11m 9s)
