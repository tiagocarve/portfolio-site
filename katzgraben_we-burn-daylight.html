we burn daylight (2009)

Released in October 2009, we burn daylight presents a collection of intimate sonic sketches captured during rehearsals for a daylight performance at the Union Chapel and the Uncommon Ground exhibition. Rather than polished studio tracks, these pieces function as raw, breathing documents of a specific time and place, blending field-imbued textures with meditative pacing, while the tracks weave ambient guitar work and evocative soundscapes that reflect the project's signature exploration of space, improvisation, and melancholic introspection.
