uncommon background (2009)

Composed and recorded specifically for the Uncommon Ground exhibition held between October and December 2009, this release explores the intersection of physical space, architecture, and long-form ambient sound. Moving away from traditional song structures, the two expansive pieces—"uncommon" and "background"—function as sonic installations, utilizing sustained electric guitar textures and subtle sample layers to shape and react to the gallery environment.
