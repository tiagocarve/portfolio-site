Welcome to my creative archive. Here you will find a curated collection of my music, books, video projects, visual art, and interactive web applications.

Based in London, my work explores themes of space, memory, and cinema across various mediums. As a sound/video artist, sound recordist, and musician, I draw on field recordings, aleatory composition, and repetition to examine the relationship between sound and place.

Through detailed soundscapes, video loops, and sound collage, my projects illuminate the imperceptible nature of everyday sounds—ranging from video-essays with ghostly audio from abandoned buildings (TSOP: Vouga ou a Moagem) to meditative s+v triptychs. As a musician, my focus centers on improvisation, ambient guitar textures, and soundtracks for imaginary films.
