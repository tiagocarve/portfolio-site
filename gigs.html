  • 1998 — Queima das Fitas : Coimbra PT [+]
    On May 8, 1998, the band Kafka performed their third-ever concert at the Queima das Fitas in Coimbra. They played on the secondary stage hosted by Rádio Universitária de Coimbra (RUC), sharing the lineup that evening with Bigo, Statement, and Ballerina. The stage was set up inside a large rectangular tent that gave the location a unique, amphitheater-like atmosphere with grassy hills inside. Meanwhile, the main stage hosted acts such as Mão Morta, Gene Loves Jezebel, and dEUS on the same night.
  • 25.04.1998 — Rockastrus : Esposende PT [+]
    On April 25, 1998, the band Kafka returned to Kastrus Bar in Forjães, Esposende, for their second-ever live performance and the grand finale of the Rockastrus music competition. The band performed a setlist featuring "A Estranheza da Alma / As Minhas Mãos / Jack", "Fürchte Dich Nicht (Manifesto Anti-Nazi)"—accompanied by the projection of images and sounds from a Hitler speech and the Nuremberg trials—"Christo", and "Je Suis Un Serf". Sharing the event with guest band Sloppy Joe and competing acts such as Swamp and Dramatic Irony, Kafka finished the competition in fourth place, and Filipe Miranda was awarded the prize for best vocalist.
  • 21.03.1998 — Rockastrus : Esposende PT [+]
    In early 1998, the band Kafka played their very first live concert at the Rockastrus music competition, held at Kastrus Bar in Forjães, Esposende. Marking the stage debut of their new drummer Pedro "Sonic" Oliveira—who joined after Falcão left following the recording of their Ab! Surdo demo—the band performed an initial setlist featuring "A Estranheza da Alma", "As Minhas Mãos / Jack", "Fürchte Dich Nicht (Manifesto Anti-Nazi)", "Christo", nd "Je Suis Un Serf". They shared the stage with Delacroix, Everground, Luís Galrito & os Matura Inculta.
