fantôme: intro das waltz (2003)

Released on CD in a digipack format by Bor Land, Fantôme – Intro Das Waltz was sound engineered by friend of the band Mariano Dias. This record was the direct result of the acoustic-leaning performance experience "Movimento Fantôme," which toured across various live dates. The album’s single, "Legacy," was accompanied by a music video directed by Rafael Lopes that aired on several European television channels. Additionally, "In Motion" had previously appeared on the Offline compilation (Fnac, 2002) and the promotional disc Kafka (2002), while "Millais: Un Modèle Pour Ofelia" was later included on the compilation Can Take You Anywhere You Want (Bor Land, 2005).
