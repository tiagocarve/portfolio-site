katzgraben - 2025 mix (2025)

A complete 2025 revision revisiting the foundational self-titled katzgraben material. This new mix strips away the dust of the original 2008 sessions, offering a profound spatial reset that brings out hidden textures in the stray guitars and ambient echoes while preserving the raw, contemplative core of the original release.
