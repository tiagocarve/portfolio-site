ab! surdo (1997)

The album ‘Ab! Surdo’ was recorded and mixed in just six hours on December 12, 1997. It was originally released as a silver-painted cassette with a silver paper cover, featuring a black ‘K’ on the plastic case and a companion booklet with lyrics. With this album, the band won a national award from Jornal de Notícias at the inaugural ‘Deixe de Ser Duro de Ouvido’ music awards in 1998. The track ‘Fürchte Dich Nicht (Manifesto Anti-Nazi)’ was subsequently featured on two compilations: Santos da Casa (CBEv, 1998) and Promusica (Promusica, 2000).
