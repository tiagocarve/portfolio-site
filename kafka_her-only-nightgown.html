her only nightgown (2000)

Recorded between 1999 and 2000 with sound engineer Michel Carvalho, the creation of Her Only Nightgown involved a winding mixing process: passing through Euterpe studio (where they chose not to mix), the SIC television studios for a first mix that was ultimately rejected by all band members, and finally returning to AMP studios where Ab! Surdo had been captured. Released as a digipack CD, it circulated via direct concert sales in late 2000 before hitting retail stores in 2001. The lyrics for the title track were adapted from the first page of Dodie Smith’s novel I Capture the Castle, which Filipe Miranda found abandoned on a street in southern Spain. Track 4 was featured on the Promusica (2001) compilation and the Kafka (2002) promotional disc, while "Gandhi's Dream Of Chocolate" soundtracked a promotional video performance at the abandoned Vouga industrial zone in Barcelos (2001).
