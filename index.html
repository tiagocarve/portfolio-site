Welcome to my creative archive—a repository for sound environments, field recordings, writing, and the spaces in between.

Working from London, I explore memory, geography, and cinematic form through field recordings, chance operations, and looping structures. Whether capturing the sonic remnants of physical sites, scoring silent films, constructing video triptychs, or designing custom digital tools, the focus remains on the relationship between audio, space, and narrative.

My musical practice moves between free improvisation, layered ambient drone textures, and scores written for unmade films. Working with guitar processing, tape-like delay systems, and chance composition, I construct slow-evolving soundscapes that sit between performance, spatial recording, and cinematic soundtracking.
