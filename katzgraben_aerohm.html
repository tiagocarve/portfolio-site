aerohm (2015)

Spanning eleven distinct movements, aerohm maps an expansive terrain of quiet introspection and shifting physical spaces. Built from sustained guitar textures, fractured melodies, and lingering electronic resonances, these pieces move fluidly between urban transit and secluded atmospheres. It stands as an intricate audio diary, capturing a refined balance between meticulous studio production and the raw warmth of analog elements.
