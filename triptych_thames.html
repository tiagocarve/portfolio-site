ttt: thames weathervane

triptychs — -scapes + meditations

This is the first draft for the triptych series. It is based on a very simple idea: three short looped videos + three looped sounds. Each video has a different duration which means that the sound piece that is constantly being generated online is always different. The sounds used in this triptych are 3 samples taken from a Katzgraben piece.
