katzgraben (2007)

Stray guitars on broken songs rocked by ambiences that appeal to introspection and contemplation. Katzgraben calls forth nostalgic memories of a traveler between Barcelos and Berlin, now living in London—a citizen of the world carrying that very cosmopolitan sense deeply wrapped up in a cinematographic tendency. Urban landscapes in counterpoint with a bucolic feeling, reunited here in a piece that has been developed over the last two years.
