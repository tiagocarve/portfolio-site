katzgraben (2007)

Stray guitars and broken song structures cradled in ambient textures that invite introspection and quiet contemplation. Katzgraben evokes the nostalgic memories of a traveler moving between Barcelos and Berlin, now based in London—a citizen of the world carrying a cosmopolitan perspective tightly wound with a cinematic sensibility. Urban landscapes dialogue with bucolic moods, captured across a collection of pieces written and recorded over the course of the previous two years.
