katzgraben (2007)

Stray guitars and fractured song structures cradle ambient soundscapes that invite deep introspection and contemplation. This self-titled work by Katzgraben channels the shifting memories of a nomadic traveler moving between Barcelos, Berlin, and London—a citizen of the world whose music carries a distinctively cosmopolitan pulse wrapped in a cinematic lens. Urban soundscapes converse with a quiet, bucolic stillness, captured across a collection of pieces developed over the course of two years.
