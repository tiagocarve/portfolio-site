ensaios 0610 (2010)

Captured live in Portugal during the June 2010 rehearsal sessions for a TSOP (The Sound of Places) performance, ensaios 0610 presents direct, unpolished documentation of sound in its rawest form. Recorded closely via a stereo portable recorder with no atmospheric room reverberations, these four sprawling extracts act as intimate acoustic artifacts—placing the listener immediately close to the instruments and highlighting the unvarnished textures, physical dynamics, and spontaneous improvisational flow of the session.
